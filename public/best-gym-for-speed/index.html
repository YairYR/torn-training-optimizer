The catch

The top of that table is mostly specialist gyms, and specialists gate on stat ratios: the stat you want to train has to sit a set percentage above your others, and you are locked out again the moment that stops being true. That makes "best" personal. George's at 7.3 dots is the ceiling if you have not built a lopsided ratio on purpose.
