What a happy jump is

Gym gains in Torn rise with your happy at the moment you train. A happy jump exploits that: you spike happy far above its normal maximum with boosters, then dump a large pile of stacked energy at the gym before the boost decays. One well-executed jump can be worth many days of normal training — at the right stat level.

The relationship between happy and gains is logarithmic (diminishing returns), which is the key to everything on this page: doubling happy does not double gains, and that is exactly why jumping eventually loses to plain energy training as stats grow.

The mechanics: caps, boosters and decay

  • The hard cap is 99,999 happy. Happy regenerates only up to your personal soft cap (your normal maximum); everything above that comes from boosters.
  • Ecstasy doubles your current happy. This is the core of every jump — stack first, double last.
  • An Erotic DVD adds 2,500 happy, and a Lollipop adds a small amount. Stacking eDVDs and then doubling with Ecstasy is how players reach the big numbers.
  • Happy above your maximum resets every 15 minutes. At xx:00, xx:15, xx:30 and xx:45 it snaps back to your normal maximum, so the whole jump — eDVDs, Ecstasy and the energy dump — has to fit inside one quarter hour.
  • The only way to hold high happy long-term is the book "Ignorance Is Bliss", which lets happy regenerate above your maximum for 31 days.
Energy side of the jump: energy regenerates about 720/day with donator (5 per 10 minutes, max 150) or about 480/day without. Xanax adds 250 energy per dose (plus 75 happy), but drugs share one cooldown of roughly 6–8 hours — about 3 doses a day total — and you can buy one 150-energy points refill per day. A jump means saving that energy up instead of spending it, which is precisely its hidden cost.

How to happy jump, step by step

  1. Stack energy. Stop training and let energy build — natural regen plus Xanax doses plus the daily refill. Most jumps stack for 24–36 hours to reach 1,000+ energy.
  2. Top up happy to your soft cap so the doubling starts from the highest possible base.
  3. Wait for a quarter-hour reset and start just after xx:00, xx:15, xx:30 or xx:45. That gives you the full 15 minutes before boosted happy snaps back.
  4. Use your Erotic DVDs. Each adds 2,500 happy above the cap — this is the stacking phase, so move quickly once you start.
  5. Pop Ecstasy last. It doubles whatever happy you have at that moment, which is why it always goes after the eDVDs, never before.
  6. Dump all stacked energy immediately at your best gym for the target stat. Boosted happy decays fast; every minute of delay is lost gains.
Overdosing is punishing: any overdose wipes 100% of your stacked energy (Vicodin excepted), and a Xanax OD also empties your happy — which can erase the entire jump you just set up.

The 99k happy jump

A "99k jump" means landing on the 99,999 cap exactly. Because Ecstasy doubles whatever you have, the target before the Ecstasy is about 50,000 happy. From a 5,000 base:

Working at a 10-star Adult Novelties company doubles each Erotic DVD to 5,000 happy, which halves the eDVD count — that is what puts 99k within reach for most players. Anything past 50,000 before the Ecstasy is wasted: the cap clips it.

Happy jump insurance

Every Xanax and the Ecstasy carry an overdose chance, and an Ecstasy OD wastes the eDVDs you just used. Happy jump insurance is a player- or faction-run deal that covers that risk: you send a small premium — commonly one Xanax — right before jumping, and if the Ecstasy overdoses, the insurer reimburses the lost items.

  • Check what is covered. Most policies cover the Ecstasy OD only, not a Xanax OD during the stacking phase.
  • Mind the window. Coverage is typically short — a couple of hours — so send the premium just before you start.
  • Keep proof. Your event log showing the overdose is what you will be asked for.

Insurance only makes sense while jumps are worth doing at all — which is the next question.

Jump vs energy training: the break-even

This is the question that actually matters, and the honest answer is: it depends on how big the stat already is.

Stat levelBetter methodWhy
Under a few 100k Happy jump Happy is the dominant term in the gain formula at low stats — big jumps give the best gain per energy by far.
Multi-million Energy training The stat's own value now drives gains. Stacking energy for 30+ hours to set up one jump wastes all the regen and drug doses you would have trained daily — the jump never pays that back.

In between, it is genuinely close and depends on your happy maximum, your perks and your energy ceiling — which is why a per-player calculation beats any rule of thumb. At high stats, the practical routine is training your full energy every day at normal happy, popping a single Ecstasy when it is affordable, rather than chasing 99,999 jumps.

When to stop happy jumping

Compare the jump against what you gave up to set it up. Over a typical ~36-hour stack:

  • The jump: about 1,150 energy (1,000 stacked plus a refill) at boosted happy.
  • Not jumping: about 2,230 energy at normal happy — four Xanax (1,000), ~1,080 of donator regen over 36 hours, and a refill (150).

Stop jumping the day the second line gains more than the first. Community rules of thumb put that around 200,000–300,000 per stat, but the real point moves with your happy maximum, your gym and your perks — enter both scenarios in the calculator with your own stats rather than trusting a fixed number.

Ignorance Is Bliss: jumping without the stack

The book Ignorance Is Bliss lets happy regenerate above your maximum for 31 days. Players use that month differently from a normal jump: instead of stacking energy for one session, they train continuously at high happy — natural regen, refills and every Xanax as it comes off cooldown. Time its start so the 31 days land while your stats are still in jump range.

FAQ

What is a happy jump in Torn?

Spiking happy far above its normal maximum — stacking Erotic DVDs, then doubling with Ecstasy, up to the 99,999 cap — and dumping stacked energy at the gym before the boost decays. Gains rise with happy, so one jump can be worth days of normal training at low stats.

Is happy jumping worth it at high stats?

Usually not. Happy scales gains logarithmically while the stat's own value scales them steadily, so once a stat is in the multi-millions, daily energy training beats sacrificing 30+ hours of regen to set up one jump. Run the comparison with your real numbers to see where you land.

How high can happy go?

99,999 is the hard ceiling. Anything above your personal maximum resets at the next quarter hour, which is why the energy dump has to happen immediately after the Ecstasy.

How many eDVDs do you need for a 99k happy jump?

Enough to reach about 50,000 before the Ecstasy: 18 at 2,500 each from a 5,000 base, or 9 at 5,000 each with a 10-star Adult Novelties job.

What is happy jump insurance?

A player- or faction-run deal: pay a small premium, usually one Xanax, just before jumping, and get the lost items back if the Ecstasy overdoses. Check what it covers and for how long.

When should I stop happy jumping?

When the ~2,230 energy you would train at normal happy during a 36-hour stack beats the ~1,150 you train at boosted happy in the jump — usually somewhere around 200k–300k per stat.

Ecstasy before or after the eDVDs?

After — always. Ecstasy doubles your current happy, so every point of happy you stack first is doubled; anything used after the Ecstasy is not.
